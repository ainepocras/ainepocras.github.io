Your Battle Plan

🏋️

Resistance Training

Lift weights, use resistance bands, or do bodyweight exercises 2-3 times per week. Progressive overload is key.

 🥗

Protein Power

Aim for 1.0-1.2g of protein per kg of body weight daily. Spread intake across meals.

 🏃

Move Every Day

Walking, swimming, cycling — keep moving. Consistency beats intensity.
😴

Rest & Recover

Muscles grow during rest. Prioritize 7-9 hours of quality sleep.

🧘

Balance & Flexibility

Yoga, tai chi, and stretching improve stability and prevent falls.

📊

Track Progress

Measure strength gains, not just weight. Celebrate small wins.
